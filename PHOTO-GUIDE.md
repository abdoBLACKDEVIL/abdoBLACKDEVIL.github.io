## For each shop listing

Collect 3–5 photographs of the **same piece**: full view, another angle, detail, and a room/scale shot. The current shop's closer views are crops of the same reference image; they are not independent photographs.

Also record:

- Title and a short description.
- Actual dimensions and materials.
- Price or a clear price-on-request policy.
- Whether the piece is available, sold, or made to order.
- Frame, mounting, or base details.
- Collection/delivery arrangements and production time, if applicable.

## Keep the photography clear

- Use soft daylight, a clean lens, and accurate colours.
- Capture straight edges without tilting the phone upward.
- Leave a little space around the piece; do not cut off frames or sculpture bases.
- Supply the original high-resolution files, preferably at least 2,000 pixels on the long edge.
- Avoid text, collages, heavy filters, and watermarks over the artwork.
- Include both portrait and landscape shots when possible.
- Keep client names, number plates, and private details out of public images unless you have permission to show them.
